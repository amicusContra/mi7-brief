## THE COIST QUESTIONS

### Q1: How did Barrett access Lawrence's expunged record?

Under MCL 780.623, an expunged record is accessible ONLY to:
- Courts
- Law enforcement
- Prosecutors
- Certain state agencies (MSP)

Barrett's campaign has no legal access. The possible pathways:

| Pathway | Likelihood | Evidence |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| **NRCC preserved pre-expungement records** | HIGH | The democratfacts.org PDF URL says "2022/06" — research prepared BEFORE automatic expungement took effect (April 11, 2023). NRCC researchers accessed the public record while it was still public and preserved it. |
| **Original MLive reporting (2013-2014)** | HIGH | MLive published the arrest and guilty plea. News coverage is not expunged. The felony was in the public record for ~10 years before expungement. |
| **ICHAT search before expungement** | MEDIUM | NRCC or Roe could have pulled ICHAT records before April 2023. |
| **Law enforcement contact** | LOW-MEDIUM | Barrett has MSP/military contacts. Roe has decades of MI GOP intelligence networks. |

**Most likely:** The NRCC pre-staged the research in 2022 when the record was still public, knowing Lawrence was a potential future candidate. The PDF was ready before Lawrence even announced.
